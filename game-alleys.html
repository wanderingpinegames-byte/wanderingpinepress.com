Rankston is known by outsiders for the big city glitz and glamour of Tempo Plaza. But away from the flickering neon, residents are stacked up like vultures on a carcass, ripping scraps where we can take them. The Department enforces a warped law and order with short tempers and fast bullets. Yet The Shiners work in the shadows to keep Kelly and his ruffians honest. We light a fire in the dark, investigating crimes undercover to ensure justice is served. To avoid the wrath of The Department, we have to move In Alleys Dark.

About the Game

In Alleys Dark is a Push Powered tabletop RPG set in the 1920s city of Rankston, a place where the law is bought, broken, or both. Players are members of The Shiners, a secret guild of justice seekers doing the work that corrupt officials won't. Everyone at the table is a player. There is no GM and no prep.

Mayor Baker and his rival Councilman Patterson sling mud at each other for the pretense of political prestige, but the real power belongs to Commissioner Kelly alone. The Department uses the violent mysteries that plague this city as impetus to incriminate anyone with the audacity to stand in their way. The Shiners are the only thing standing between Rankston's residents and the people sworn to protect them.

The World of Rankston

Rankston is a city of sharp contrasts. The glittering lights of Tempo Plaza draw the wealthy and the ambitious, while the rest of the city scrapes by in the shadows of those same towers. Three forces shape life here:

  • The Department - Commissioner Kelly's police force, more interested in power than justice
  • The Political Machine - Mayor Baker and Councilman Patterson, trading favors and accusations in equal measure
  • The Shiners - a secret guild of ordinary people doing extraordinary things in the dark

Is This Game for You?

In Alleys Dark is for players who love morally complex stories, 1920s atmosphere, and the satisfaction of justice served outside the law. It is not a game of flashy combat or dungeon crawls. It is a game of investigation, difficult choices, and finding out what your characters are willing to do when the system has failed everyone around them.

If you love noir fiction, stories about ordinary people standing up to corrupt power, and collaborative storytelling with no prep required, this game was made for you.
