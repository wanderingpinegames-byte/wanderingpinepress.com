We're the Hazzards

We're the husband and wife team behind Wandering Pine Press. We live in Virginia with two wild sons, two lazy cats, and a lot of love. As an independent tabletop RPG publisher, we focus on games that help players collaborate to tell compelling character-driven stories. Thank you for taking an interest in our craft.

Brian is the creative force behind the mechanical and narrative systems in our games. As a collector of hobbies, he has studied boardgames, gamebooks, RPGs, papercraft, and magic tricks, and is always searching for another source of obsession.

Crystal is the steady and disciplined hand, and the keen eye behind our design and layout. As a multimedia artist with a particular mastery of crochet, she has a knack for simple and beautiful aesthetics.
